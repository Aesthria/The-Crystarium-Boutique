Thank you for helping improve The Crystarium Boutique.

This is an inbound-equals-outbound contribution policy.

You retain copyright in your contribution. The project does not require a Contributor License Agreement or copyright assignment.

Only submit work you created or have the legal right to contribute under these terms. Clearly identify third-party material and its source and license; do not include proprietary game assets or code copied from another project without permission.

## Development basics

- Keep changes focused and preserve the project's local-first, offline-runtime design.
- Build and test with the toolchain documented in [README.md](README.md) and [DEPENDENCIES.md](DEPENDENCIES.md).
- Update documentation and third-party notices when a change adds or alters a dependency, data source, IPC contract, or packaged asset.
- Do not commit local build output, developer configuration, credentials, or generated staging artifacts.
